## FAQ

### Can I fork and modify WORM Engines under BSL?

Yes. You can modify it for your own use. You cannot distribute your modifications as a competing product without a license.

### What happens to my data when WORM goes AGPL?

Your data is yours. The license change does not affect your existing deployments. You can continue running WORM under BSL until December 31, 2027.

### Do I need a license to use WORM in a private network?

No. You can run WORM internally without a commercial license.

### What about open-source projects?

WORM is not currently under an open-source license (BSL 1.1 is source-available, not OSI-approved). After December 31, 2027, AGPL-3.0-only applies.

**Today:** Open-source projects should either wait until 2028 or contact sales for evaluation-tier terms.

### Can I use WORM in a closed-source product?

Not under BSL 1.1. A commercial license is required. Contact licensing@snapkittywest.dev.

### What if I build a SaaS product using WORM?

That requires a commercial license. Contact sales to discuss terms.
